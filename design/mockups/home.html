VART synthetic demo data

Questionnaires answered from your own documents

VART reads a company’s policies, reports and records, answers each item of a vendor security questionnaire, and quotes the exact source line behind every answer. Labels are decided by rules in code, not by the model, and a person approves every answer before export.

  • sTry with a sample companyopen

    Kestrelyn, a fictional SaaS company: 22 documents and the bundled Vendor Security Questionnaire. The run is precomputed, so it opens instantly and costs nothing.
  • oUse your own filesupload

    An xlsx or csv questionnaire, plus documents in docx, pdf, md or xlsx. Your workspace is private, stops working after 24 hours and is deleted within about 37.

how a label is decided

labelwhenvalue
verifiedEvery surviving quote says the same thing, from a final document.Yes / No
partialMixed evidence, a negation in the quote, a scope difference, or draft documents only.Partial
conflictTwo documents disagree. A newer record is listed first, and you are asked which is current.—
unknownNo evidence survived the checks. The item goes to Questions for you.—
confirmed by youYou answered it; your answer is kept as a dated statement and cited.Yours
not applicableYou marked it so, with a reason in the audit log.N/A

A quote that is not found word for word in its source, or that comes from a template, a contract or a flagged injection, is dropped before any label is set. Every answer stays “Draft, not approved” until a person approves it.
