# Vulnerability Management

Kestrelyn, Inc. - Version 1.0 - Effective 2026-03-01

This policy sets out how Kestrelyn finds, tracks and fixes vulnerabilities in the systems that make up Kestrelyn Ledger.

## Policy Statements

Kestrelyn policy requires that:

(a) All vulnerability findings must be reported and tracked to resolution.

(b) Findings are prioritized by severity and by how easily they can be exploited.

## Vulnerability Scanning

Authenticated vulnerability scans of all production hosts and container images run weekly.

## Remediation Timelines

Critical vulnerabilities are patched within 14 days of discovery.

Vulnerabilities in third-party libraries and dependencies are remediated within the same timelines as other findings.

## Independent Testing

An independent third party performs a penetration test of Kestrelyn Ledger annually.

## Customer Testing

Customer-initiated penetration testing is not permitted.
