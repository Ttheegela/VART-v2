## 1. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Services | The Kestrelyn Ledger software-as-a-service application and the related support described in an Order Form. |
| Order Form | A document signed by both parties that lists the Services, the subscription term, the fees and any special terms. |

| Security Incident | A confirmed breach of security that leads to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration or unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Data. |

## 2. Services

Provider will make the Services available to Customer under this Agreement and each Order Form. Provider may update the Services from time to time, provided that no update materially reduces the functionality Customer purchased.

## 3. Fees and Payment

Customer will pay the fees stated in the Order Form within 45 days of the invoice date. Fees are invoiced in advance for each subscription term and are non-cancellable except as stated in this Agreement.

## 4. Term and Termination

This Agreement starts on the effective date and continues until all Order Forms have expired or been terminated. Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within 60 days of written notice.

## 5. Customer Data

Customer owns Customer Data and grants Provider the rights needed to operate the Services.

## 6. Security Incidents

Provider shall notify Customer within 72 hours of a confirmed Security Incident.

The parties will cooperate in good faith to address any Security Incident.

## 7. Service Availability

Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain 99.9% availability.

## 8. Warranties and Disclaimers

Each party represents that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement. Except as stated in this Agreement, the Services are provided as is.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Neither party's total liability under this Agreement will exceed the fees paid or payable by Customer under the applicable Order Form in the year before the claim arose.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least the care it uses for its own, and will use it only to perform this Agreement.

## 11. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

## 12. General

This Agreement, together with each Order Form, is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. Customer may not assign this Agreement without Provider's prior written consent.
