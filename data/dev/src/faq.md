# Kestrelyn Security FAQ

Answers to the questions customers ask us most often. Last updated 2026-08-20.

## About Kestrelyn Ledger

### What is Kestrelyn Ledger?

Kestrelyn Ledger is a web application that helps finance teams capture, approve and pay supplier invoices.

### Who builds Kestrelyn Ledger?

Kestrelyn, Inc. is a software company based in Austin, Texas, with about 85 employees.

### Which accounting systems does Kestrelyn Ledger connect to?

Kestrelyn Ledger has standard integrations with the most common general ledger and payment systems. Your customer success manager can confirm the current list for your account.

### Does Kestrelyn Ledger have an API?

Yes. Kestrelyn Ledger has a REST API for creating invoices and reading approval status. Administrators can find the documentation under Settings.

### Which languages does the web app support?

The web app is available in English and Spanish, and each user can choose a language in their profile.

### Can I try Kestrelyn Ledger before I buy?

Yes. We offer a guided pilot with a sample set of invoices. Ask your account manager to arrange one.

### What happens to approved invoices after a payment run?

Approved invoices are exported as a payment file and marked as paid when your bank confirms the payment.

### Can I export my invoices?

Yes. Administrators can export invoices, approvals and payment runs as CSV files from the Reports page whenever they need to.

## Accounts and Billing

### How are subscriptions priced?

Subscriptions are priced per active approver, with a platform fee for each connected entity. Your order form lists the exact fees.

### Where do I find the invoices Kestrelyn sends me?

Open Settings and choose Billing. Invoices and receipts for every subscription term are listed there as PDF files.

### Who can change the plan on my account?

Any administrator of your organization can request a change from the Billing page, and the change takes effect on the next billing date.

## Vulnerability Reports

### How do I report a security vulnerability?

Security researchers can report vulnerabilities to vulnerability-reports@kestrelyn.example, and Kestrelyn acknowledges each report within two business days.
