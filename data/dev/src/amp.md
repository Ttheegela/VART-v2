# Asset Inventory Management

Kestrelyn, Inc. - Version 1.0 - Effective 2026-01-15

You can't protect what you can't see. Therefore, visibility into both the physical and the digital assets of Kestrelyn is imperative.

## Policy Statements

Kestrelyn maintains an inventory of its information assets.

Kestrelyn policy requires that:

(a) All assets must have an identified owner.

(b) All physical assets must be labeled with a company property tag.

## Retired Assets

Storage media from retired assets, including media that held production data, is wiped following NIST SP 800-88 guidelines or physically destroyed before reuse or disposal.
