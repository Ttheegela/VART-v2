# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery

Kestrelyn, Inc. - Version 1.0 - Effective 2026-01-15

The Kestrelyn Contingency Plan establishes procedures to recover Kestrelyn following a disruption resulting from a disaster. This Disaster Recovery Policy is maintained by the Kestrelyn Security team.

## Policy Statements

Kestrelyn policy requires that:

(a) A plan and process for business continuity and disaster recovery (BCDR) must be defined and documented.

(b) Metrics shall be measured and identified recovery enhancements shall be filed to improve the BCDR process.

(c) Security controls and requirements must be maintained during all BCDR activities.

## Recovery Data

Kestrelyn performs daily backups of all production databases.

Backup restores are tested quarterly.
