# Marrowgate Trust Center FAQ

Answers to the questions customers ask us most often. Last updated 2026-09-01.

## About Marrowgate Payroll

### What is Marrowgate Payroll?

Marrowgate Payroll is a cloud service that calculates pay, produces payslips and files payroll taxes for employers.

### Who builds it?

Marrowgate Labs Ltd is a software company based in Dublin with about 140 employees.

### Which countries are supported?

Ireland, the United Kingdom, Germany and the Netherlands are supported today. Your customer operations manager can confirm the current list.

### Is there an API?

Yes. Marrowgate Payroll has a REST API for submitting pay inputs and reading pay run status.

## Signing In

Customers can sign in to Marrowgate Payroll through SAML single sign-on with their own identity provider, at no extra charge.

## Vulnerability Reports

### How do I report a security vulnerability?

Security researchers can report vulnerabilities to security-reports@marrowgate.example, and Marrowgate acknowledges each report within three business days.
