# Asset Lifecycle Policy

Marrowgate Labs Ltd - Version 0.4 - DRAFT - not approved

DRAFT: this policy is still being written by IT Operations and has not been approved.

## Tracking

Marrowgate maintains an inventory of its information assets.

Each asset has an owner who is responsible for it until it is retired.

## Retirement

Storage media from retired assets is wiped to NIST SP 800-88 standards or physically destroyed before it is reused or disposed of.

Retirement is recorded against the asset.
