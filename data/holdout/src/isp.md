# Information Security Program

Marrowgate Labs Ltd - Version 2.1 - Effective 2026-02-02

Marrowgate builds payroll software, so the data our customers entrust to us is among the most sensitive that a company holds. This program sets out how we protect it. It applies to every employee, contractor and system that supports Marrowgate Payroll.

Security at Marrowgate rests on two principles: protect people's pay data as if it were our own, and make the safe way the easy way.

## Program Statements

Marrowgate maintains a documented information security program that has been approved by the CEO.

The information security program is owned by Ilse Brandvold, Head of Security.

Marrowgate security policies are reviewed once a year, and sooner after a significant change.

## Certifications

Marrowgate does not hold ISO/IEC 27001 certification.
