# Supplier Risk Policy

Marrowgate Labs Ltd - Version 1.1 - Effective 2026-03-02

Scope: this policy applies to vendors and contractors who supply services to Marrowgate or work on its behalf.

## Supplier Assessment

Every vendor with access to customer data is assessed for security risk at least once a year.

Marrowgate publishes its subprocessor list and gives customers 45 days' notice before adding a subprocessor.

## Contractor Staff

Marrowgate does not run background checks on contractor staff; vendors confirm that their own staff are screened.

Contractors receive access only to the systems named in their statement of work.
