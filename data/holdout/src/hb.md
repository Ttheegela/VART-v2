## Welcome

Marrowgate builds Marrowgate Payroll. We are about 140 people working from our Dublin office and from home across Europe.

## Working Hours and Time Off

Most teams share core hours between 10:00 and 15:00 in their local time. Agree planned time off with your manager and add it to the team calendar.

## Equipment

Marrowgate provides each employee with a laptop and the accessories they need.

Every company laptop must have its disk fully encrypted before it is issued.

Install only software that IT Operations has approved. Tell IT Operations if you need a replacement or a repair.

## Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed when you submit a receipt through the expense tool within the same month.

## Code of Conduct

We treat each other with respect. Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are not tolerated.
