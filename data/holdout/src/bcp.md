# Resilience Runbook

Marrowgate Labs Ltd - Version 2.0 - Effective 2026-04-02

This runbook tells the on-call team how to restore Marrowgate Payroll after a major failure.

## Objectives

The recovery time objective (RTO) is 4 hours and the recovery point objective (RPO) is 1 hour.

## Exercises

Restores from backup are tested twice a year; the most recent test was completed in February 2026.

Failover to the secondary AWS region is tested once a year; the last test was completed in March 2026.

## Communication During an Outage

The incident lead posts status updates to customers every 60 minutes during a major outage.

Customers are notified of a confirmed breach within 5 business days.

## Roles

The CTO is the executive owner of this runbook. The IT Operations Lead keeps the contact list current.
