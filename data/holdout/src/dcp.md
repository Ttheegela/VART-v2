# Data Handling Policy

Marrowgate Labs Ltd - Version 1.2 - Effective 2026-02-02

This policy describes how Marrowgate labels, stores and disposes of data. It applies to all data handled by Marrowgate staff and systems.

## Classification

Marrowgate classifies data as Open, Protected or Restricted.

Payroll records, bank details and tax identifiers are always Restricted. Marketing content is Open. Everything else is Protected unless the data owner decides otherwise.

## Storage

Marrowgate Payroll stores all customer data in AWS eu-west-1 (Ireland).

Restricted data must not be copied to personal devices or personal cloud accounts.

## End of Contract

Customer data is deleted within 60 days after the contract ends.

## Machine Learning

Marrowgate does not use customer payroll data to train machine learning models.
