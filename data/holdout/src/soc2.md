# SOC 2 Type II Report Summary

Marrowgate Labs Ltd - Examination period: 2025-10-01 to 2026-03-31

Scope: this report covers the production environment.

This summary describes the independent service auditor's examination of the Marrowgate Payroll production environment and the main results of that examination. It does not replace the full report.

## Independent Service Auditor's Opinion

The service auditor issued an unqualified opinion on the Company's SOC 2 Type II examination for the period 2025-10-01 to 2026-03-31.

The examination covered the Security and Availability categories of the AICPA trust services criteria. A Type II examination tests whether controls were suitably designed and operated effectively throughout the period.

## System Description

Marrowgate Labs Ltd (the Company) provides Marrowgate Payroll, a cloud-based service that calculates pay, produces payslips and files payroll taxes for employers.

### Infrastructure

All production infrastructure is hosted in Amazon Web Services.

### People

The Company has 140 employees, organized into engineering, product, customer operations, security and IT, and finance and people teams.

## System Operations

Security logs are kept for 6 months.

## Exceptions Noted

The auditor noted one exception during testing.

For 3 of 40 leavers sampled, access was removed 4 days after the leaving date.

Management's response to the exception is included in the full report.

## Other Information Provided by the Company

The following information was provided by the Company. It is not covered by the auditor's opinion.

The Company does not operate a public bug bounty program.
