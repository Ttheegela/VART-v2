# Incident Response Policy

Marrowgate Labs Ltd - Version 1.0 - Effective 2026-03-09

This policy describes how Marrowgate prepares for and handles security incidents.

## Preparation

Marrowgate maintains a documented incident response plan.

An incident response tabletop exercise is run every year.

## Response

The on-call engineer opens an incident record, assigns an incident lead and starts a timeline. The incident lead decides when to involve the CEO and the People Director.

## Customer Communication

Affected customers are notified within 72 hours of a confirmed breach.

Communication to customers is approved by the incident lead and the CEO before it is sent.

## After an Incident

The incident lead writes up what happened and what will change, and the Head of Security tracks each follow-up action until it is closed.
