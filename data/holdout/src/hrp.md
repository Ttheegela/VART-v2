# People Security Policy

Marrowgate Labs Ltd - Version 1.2 - Effective 2026-02-02

Scope: this policy applies to employees.

## Joining

Every employee passes a background check before their first day.

All employees complete security awareness training when they join and every year after.

## Engineering Staff

Developers complete secure coding training every year that covers authorization bypass, session management, injection, cross-site scripting and cross-site request forgery.

## Leaving

When an employee leaves, the People team tells IT Operations the same day, and equipment is returned within five working days.
