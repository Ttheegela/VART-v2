# Vulnerability and Patching Policy

Marrowgate Labs Ltd - Version 1.3 - Effective 2026-03-16

This policy sets how Marrowgate finds and fixes weaknesses in its systems and in Marrowgate Payroll.

## Finding Weaknesses

Authenticated vulnerability scans cover all production hosts, containers and cloud accounts every week.

Marrowgate commissions an independent penetration test of Marrowgate Payroll every year.

Customers may not run penetration tests against Marrowgate Payroll.

## Fixing Weaknesses

Critical vulnerabilities are patched within 7 days of discovery.

Critical vulnerabilities in third-party libraries are fixed within the same time limits.

[Company Name] must remediate critical findings within [number] days.

High findings are scheduled into the next sprint and tracked until closed.
