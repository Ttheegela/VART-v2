# Continuity Policy

Marrowgate Labs Ltd - Version 1.0 - Effective 2026-02-02

Payroll cannot wait, so Marrowgate plans for the loss of a system, a region or a supplier.

## Data Protection

Marrowgate takes a full backup of every production database each day.

Restores from backup are tested every quarter.

## Roles

The CTO owns the continuity plan. The IT Operations Lead runs the exercises.
